# Governance

## Maintainers

bashy is maintained by DHNT Inc. The current maintainer is
[@qiangli](https://github.com/qiangli), who has merge and release rights.
The project intends to add maintainers; a contributor becomes one after a
sustained record of reviewed contributions and agreement from the current
maintainers.

## Decisions

Day-to-day changes are decided in pull requests. Larger changes (language
behavior, compatibility guarantees, the release and versioning policy,
licensing) are proposed in an issue first and stay open for comment for at
least a week before a maintainer decides. Decisions and their reasons are
recorded in the issue or in `docs/`.

## Compatibility and releases

Compatibility targets are GNU Bash 5.3 and POSIX.1-2016. Versioning and
support rules are in
[docs/release-roadmap-and-versioning.md](docs/release-roadmap-and-versioning.md);
security fixes follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## License and contributions

bashy is BSD-3-Clause. Contributions are accepted under the same license.
Dependencies must stay permissive-licensed; see
[docs/licensing-supply-chain-policy.md](docs/licensing-supply-chain-policy.md).

## Conduct

Be respectful and assume good faith. Report conduct issues to the maintainers
privately.

## Continuity

If the maintainer becomes unable to continue, DHNT Inc. holds the repository
and release credentials and will appoint a successor or transfer the project
to a neutral home.
