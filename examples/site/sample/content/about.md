## Our Mission

Acme Innovations was founded with a clear objective: to restore autonomy and resilience to software infrastructure. In an era where complex hosted clouds dominate, we believe developers and organizations should retain complete control over their code, their deployment environments, and their data.

## Engineering Philosophy

- **Simplicity over cleverness**: Predictable systems with clean boundaries outlast intricate monoliths.
- **Permissive Open Source**: Every tool we build and deliver stands on battle-tested permissive foundations (Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD).
- **Human Guidance, Agentic Execution**: People set direction and approve decisions; autonomous agents handle repetitive mechanical execution safely.

## Leadership

Our engineering team comprises veteran systems programmers, distributed systems specialists, and tool builders with decades of combined experience across open-source infrastructure.
