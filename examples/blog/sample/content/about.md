---
title: About this journal
---

A self-hosted journal. The posts are plain Markdown files under `content/posts/`,
edited in any text editor or through the bundled admin CMS at `/admin/`, built
into a static site by a self-contained generator, and served from this machine.

Media in a post lives next to the text: a video or voice take with captions, a
recorded terminal scene, a diagram, an equation. Nothing here calls out to the
network to render.
